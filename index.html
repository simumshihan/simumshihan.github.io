01 — ABOUT

GIS professional with an environmental perspective.

I am a GIS Analyst with an academic background in Geography and Environment and a focus on Geographic Information System and Remote Sensing. My work involves spatial analysis, satellite imagery, raster processing, cartography and environmental applications of GIS.

I am particularly interested in using geospatial technologies to investigate climate and environmental change, disaster risk, urban environments and sustatinable development. I work with ArcGIS, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, Landsat, Sentinel-2 and DEM-based spatial analysis.
